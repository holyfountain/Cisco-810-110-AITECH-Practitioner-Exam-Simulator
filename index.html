Cisco Employee Discount

Book the Real Exam - 810-110

The official process for Cisco employees to request an exam discount and schedule the 810-110 Cisco AI Technical Practitioner (Specialist) exam. Manager approval is required and your department is cross-charged, so your out-of-pocket cost is $0.

Cost to you$0 ($50 dept cross-charge)
Discount validity30 days from issue
EligibilityRegular full-time employees
DeliveryTest center or online (OnVUE)

1. Before you start

1.1Manager approval: get your manager's approval for the department cross-charge before requesting a discount. Red-badge contractors and temporary employees are not eligible.

Sample manager approval email

Subject: Approval Request: Cisco Certification Exam Discount (810-110)

Hi [Manager Name],

I have completed my preparation for the 810-110: Cisco AI Technical Practitioner exam (Specialist level) and would like to request an employee exam discount to take the test. Under the Cisco Certifications Exam Employee Discount Program, my department is cross-charged for the discount, so I need your approval before I submit the request.

Please let me know if you approve so I can proceed.

Thank you,
[Your Name]

1.2Legal name: make sure your profile shows your legal name exactly as on your government photo ID - a mismatch means you cannot test and forfeit the fee.

1.3One at a time: only request a discount when you are ready to test within 30 days - it cannot be extended or re-issued.

2. Request the exam discount

2.1Open the Certification Exams page on the Internal IT Training and Certifications SharePoint - Cisco's hub where full-time employees and contractors get authorized training and certification exams at low or no cost. Beyond exam discounts, it also links to self-paced prep and hands-on labs on Cisco U., instructor-led training, and continuing-education (recertification) resources.

2.2Under Certification written exams ($50 department cross-charge per exam), click Register written exams here to open the discount request portal.

2.3Select the Specialist Exams tab, then under Business click 810-110 Cisco AI Technical Practitioner.

2.4Confirm the selected exam - your name, CEC ID, and email are pre-filled.

2.5Enter your CSCO ID if you have one (from a previous Cisco score report or Cisco email); leave it blank to have a new ID generated.

2.6Complete your mailing address and phone (all required) and click Next.

2.7Tick the Terms & Conditions box (consent to share your details with Pearson VUE) and click Request Discount.

2.8You will receive your discount code by email once the request is processed.

3. Schedule with Pearson VUE

3.1Sign in to Cisco CertMetrics (the Cisco Certification Tracking System) and, from the Dashboard, click Schedule an Exam.

3.2Confirm your legal name, then click Schedule Now (Cisco Exams) - you will be taken to Pearson VUE.

3.3Search for 810-110: Cisco AI Technical Practitioner and open the Proctored Exams group.

3.4Choose In person at a test center or Online with OnVUE.

3.5Pick a date and time within your 30-day discount window.

3.6Apply your discount code so the balance is $0.00, then confirm. Your discount email explains exactly how to redeem the code.

4. Policy notes

4.1You may hold only one active discount per exam at a time, and must wait until it is used or expired before requesting another.

4.2The discount is valid for 30 days from issue and cannot be extended or re-issued.

4.3Missed appointments ("no-shows") forfeit the discount.
