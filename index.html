Cisco Employee Discount

Book the Real Exam - 810-110

The official process for Cisco employees to request an exam discount and schedule the 810-110 Cisco AI Technical Practitioner (Specialist) exam. Manager approval is required and your department is cross-charged, so your out-of-pocket cost is $0.

Cost to you$0 (department cross-charge)
Discount validity30 days from issue
EligibilityRegular full-time employees
DeliveryTest center or online (OnVUE)

1. Before you start

  1. Manager approval: get your manager's approval for the department cross-charge before requesting a discount. Red-badge contractors and temporary employees are not eligible.
  2. Legal name: make sure your profile shows your legal name exactly as on your government photo ID - a mismatch means you cannot test and forfeit the fee.
  3. One at a time: only request a discount when you are ready to test within 30 days - it cannot be extended or re-issued.

2. Request the exam discount

  1. Open the Cisco Certifications Exam Employee Discount Program (via Cisco.com → Training & Events).
  2. Select the Specialist Exams tab, then under Business click 810-110 Cisco AI Technical Practitioner.
  3. Confirm the selected exam - your name, CEC ID, and email are pre-filled.
  4. Enter your CSCO ID if you have one (from a previous Cisco score report or Cisco email); leave it blank to have a new ID generated.
  5. Complete your mailing address and phone (all required) and click Next.
  6. Tick the Terms & Conditions box (consent to share your details with Pearson VUE) and click Request Discount.
  7. You will receive your discount code by email once the request is processed.

3. Schedule with Pearson VUE

  1. Sign in to Cisco CertMetrics (the Cisco Certification Tracking System) and, from the Dashboard, click Schedule an Exam.
  2. Confirm your legal name, then click Schedule Now (Cisco Exams) - you will be taken to Pearson VUE.
  3. Search for 810-110: Cisco AI Technical Practitioner and open the Proctored Exams group.
  4. Choose In person at a test center or Online with OnVUE.
  5. Pick a date and time within your 30-day discount window.
  6. Apply your discount code so the balance is $0.00, then confirm. Your discount email explains exactly how to redeem the code.

Policy notes

  • You may hold only one active discount per exam at a time, and must wait until it is used or expired before requesting another.
  • The discount is valid for 30 days from issue and cannot be extended or re-issued.
  • Missed appointments ("no-shows") forfeit the discount.
